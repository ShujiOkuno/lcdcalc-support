Retro Calc collects no personal information and no usage data whatsoever. The app does not connect to the internet.

The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, including your name, email address, location, contacts, or usage activity. Nothing you enter into the calculator leaves your device, and no record of it is kept.

The app does not connect to the internet. It performs no communication with external servers, no analytics, and no ad delivery.

3. Disclosure to Third Parties

Because no information is collected, there is nothing to share with third parties. The app uses no third-party analytics tools and no advertising SDKs.

4. Children’s Privacy

The app is suitable for users of all ages. It collects no information of any kind, including information about age.

5. Permissions

The app requests no access to device features or data such as the camera, microphone, photos, location, or contacts.
